RAM, ROM And Expandable: 4GB RAM with 128GB internal storage gives Galaxy A07 5G a balanced setup for calling, streaming, messaging and daily apps, while the hybrid SIM tray also supports microSD expansion up to 2TB when you need extra space for photos, videos and files. The exact storage type is not specified in the provided India sources, so it is not claimed here
Display: Galaxy A07 5G features a 6.7 inch PLS LCD with HD+ 720 x 1600 resolution and a 120Hz refresh rate, combining a large viewing area with smoother scrolling in everyday use. The listed brightness goes up to 800 nits on the India sources, helping with visibility for browsing, video playback and social media use in brighter outdoor surroundings
Camera: The camera setup includes a 50MP main rear camera paired with a 2MP depth camera for portraits, plus an 8MP front camera for selfies and video calls. Samsung also lists autofocus on the main camera, digital zoom up to 10x, FHD video recording at 30fps and HD slow motion at 120fps, covering the core capture modes needed for day to day use
Processor: Galaxy A07 5G is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 6300 octa-core processor, with Samsung stating 2.4 GHz and 2.0 GHz CPU speeds and a 6nm design for responsive everyday performance. The India sources consistently position it for smooth handling of regular apps, streaming, browsing and daily multitasking without adding unsupported benchmark or gaming claims
Battery: Samsung Galaxy A07 5G packs a 6000mAh battery, with Samsung noting a 5830mAh rated minimum value, while the India marketplace listings mention 25W fast charging support. This combination is aimed at longer daily use across calls, browsing, video and apps, and the in-box contents listed on the India pages include a C to C data cable rather than a charger adapter
Build And Durability: The phone carries a slim 8.2mm profile and 199g weight, giving it a large-screen form without becoming overly bulky for routine handling. India sources also mention ABS plastic body construction and IP54 spill and splash resistance, while Samsung describes a redesigned rear camera layout and confirms Black, Light Violet and Light Green finish options for the model
Software And Updates: Galaxy A07 5G runs Android 16 with One UI 8, and Samsung states that the device is eligible for 6 generations of OS upgrades plus 6 years of security updates from the first global launch date. Samsung also highlights One UI 8 usability improvements and Quick Share support, making the software package more current for long-term everyday use
